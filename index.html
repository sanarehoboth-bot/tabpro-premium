✋ The 9 Times Table Finger Trick

Hold up both hands, fingers spread. Number them 1 to 10, left to right.
To find 9 × n, tap finger number n below — that finger "bends down."
Fingers to its left = TENS digit, fingers to its right = ONES digit.

Tap a finger to try it!
Example: tap finger 4 → 3 fingers left (30) + 6 fingers right (6) = 9 × 4 = 36
